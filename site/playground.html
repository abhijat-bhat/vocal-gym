Raga and tala, with a creativity dial

Three generators that cannot break the rules of raga or tala, and a judge for jathis you write yourself. Temperature is the performer's creativity: 0 keeps to patterns a listener recognises, 2 reaches for unconventional ones. No setting can produce a wrong one.

        Playback uses equal temperament and plain tones, so it shows swaras and timing, not gamakas. Temperature is creativity: 0 keeps to the most recognisable choices, 2 favours unconventional ones. The rules hold at every setting.

        How to use it

        Alankaras

        • The first pattern in each of the 35 talas is the line from the 35 tala alankara sheet.
        • New pattern gives one you have not seen for this raga and tala; Previous and Next step through them.
        • Janya ragas get the sheet line adapted; swaras changed to keep the raga's direction rules are underlined.

        Jathis

        • Generate: set tala, nadai, avartanams, eduppu and yati. Open in editor shows the jathi as JathiScript.
        • Compose and judge: write your own. The gate checks the arithmetic; six craft scores total 100; the first suggestion says which line to fix.

        Kalpana swaras

        • Choose the raga, the length, the opening swara and the swara the composition resumes on. The round ends on the raga's swara just below or just above it, so you step straight into the eduppu.
        • Violet swaras come from unconventional devices, teal from the mora. Raise the temperature to hear more of them.
