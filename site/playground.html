Raga and tala, with a creativity dial

Two generators that cannot break the rules of raga or tala, and a lab that teaches how moras, tihais and korvais are counted to land, in any of the 35 talas. Creativity 0 keeps to what a listener recognises; 2 reaches for the unconventional. No setting can produce a wrong one.

    How this round was built

      The 72 melakartas as a system: each number gives its swaras, and by the katapayadi rule the first two syllables of each name give the number. Pick one to see both worked out, decode any name, or build a scale and find its mela.

      How a mora, tihai or korvai is counted so that it lands on sam, in any of the 35 talas: learn it step by step, build your own, then check it in the judge.

      The jathi judge reads JathiScript (one word is one matra), checks that it adds up and lands, and scores its craft. Korvais from the lab open here already written out.

      How to use it

      Alankaras

      • The first pattern in each of the 35 talas is the line from the 35 tala alankara sheet.
      • New pattern gives one you have not seen for this raga and tala; Previous and Next step through them.
      • Janya ragas get the sheet line adapted; swaras changed to keep the raga's direction rules are underlined.

      Korvai & tihai lab

      • Choose your tala (any of the 35, or a chapu), its gati and kalai once, at the top; every lesson and result follows it.
      • Learn: nine short lessons, from counting the cycle to the korvai and the tihai of tihais, each live for your tala. Build: any form, span, start and eduppu, with every solution listed and checked.
      • Check: the jathi judge. A korvai you built opens there written out as JathiScript.

      Kalpana swaras

      • Choose the raga, the length, the opening swara and the swara the composition resumes on. The round ends on the raga's swara just below or just above it, so you step straight into the eduppu.
      • Violet swaras come from unconventional devices, teal from the mora. Raise the temperature to hear more of them.
